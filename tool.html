Editing the database

Click any row to edit it, or add a new project. Edits are kept in your browser until they are published, and rows you changed are marked on the left.

Publishing changes for everyone

  1. Click Download CSV (or Copy CSV).
  2. In the GitHub repository, open data/projects.csv, choose Edit, paste or upload the new file and commit.
  3. The site updates for everyone within a minute or two.

Field conventions

Ranges use a dash (35-49). Capacity may be in MW (806) or GW (1.2 GW). Several foundation types are separated with ;. Phase is one of 1 - Concept, 2 - Early development, 3 - Under development, 4 - Under construction or 5 - Operational.
